Design rules for the extended version

Simple enough for every parent, complete enough to replace five other channels

1

Words with every icon

No icon stands alone. Every button has a label, and parents can switch the whole app to বাংলা.

2

Today comes first

Home shows what changed today for this child. Everything else is one tap away in All services.

3

One clear action

Large buttons, one main action per card, and plain words such as “Pay now” and “Say yes”.

4

Pay the familiar way

Bank card or internet banking in two taps, with a clear status so no one pays twice.

5

Voice is welcome

Parents can send a short voice message, just as they do on WhatsApp. Typing is optional.

6

Light on data, SMS as backup

Screens load fast on slow mobile data. Urgent alerts also go by SMS to parents who have not opened the app.

Ideas borrowed from global school apps

Class photo stories, as in ClassDojo · One inbox with SMS backup, as in ParentSquare · Message translation, as in Remind

Left out on purpose

Parent-to-parent group chats, likes and comments, complex charts, and AI chatbots in the first release. These add noise and support load for the school.
