One connected school app for parents, teachers and the office

The school enters something once. Parents see it, reply in one tap, and the school sees the reply.

For parents · mobile app

Parent app

  • Today: attendance, homework, lunch, bus
  • Pay fees by bank card or internet banking
  • Results, notices, calendar and consent
  • Chat with teachers, leave, meetings, feedback
  • Safe pickup pass and emergency alerts

For teachers · mobile app

Teacher app

  • Attendance in under a minute
  • Homework, diary and class photos
  • Marks entry and leave approvals
  • Parent messages with quiet hours
  • Sign in with phone, no password

For the office · web panel

Admin panel

  • Class → section → day, then input
  • Fees, results, routine and meals
  • Notices with SMS backup and read tracking
  • Emergency alert, safe pickup at the gate
  • Fee collection with automatic reminders
